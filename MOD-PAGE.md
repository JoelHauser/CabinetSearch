# Cabinet Looter

Open any drawer of a filing cabinet and loot the whole cabinet from one window.

No more open, search, close, next drawer. Open any drawer and all of that cabinet's drawers show
up side by side, and they get searched one after another.

<!-- screenshot goes here -->

## What it does

- All drawers of the cabinet in one loot window, each with its own grid
- Searches them one by one at normal speed, using your Search and Attention skills
- Each drawer shows its state: not searched, searching, empty, or how many items it holds
- The SEARCHING bar stays up until the whole cabinet is done; X stops it
- Close the window and come back later: it picks up where it left off
- **Optional:** loot several cabinets standing side by side from one window (off by default)

## What it doesn't do

- It doesn't change the loot. Nothing is rerolled, duplicated, deleted or moved to you
  automatically.
- It doesn't skip searching. Items still turn up one by one.

## Install

Unzip over your SPT folder. Client only, no server part. Settings are in the F12 menu.

For SPT 4.0.x (backport). SPT 4.1.x players: use the main release.

## Good to know

- Empty drawers in a scav raid are SPT, not this mod. When SPT cuts a scav raid's loot by half or
  more, every drawer on the map spawns empty.
- Fika: not tested yet.
